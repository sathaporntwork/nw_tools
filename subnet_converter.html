Subnet Converter

Subnet Mask to CIDR

Format: network_ip subnet_mask (e.g., 10.0.0.0 255.255.255.0)

CIDR to Subnet Mask

Format: x.x.x.x/y (IPv4 address with CIDR prefix)

CIDR to Wildcard Mask

Format: x.x.x.x/y (IPv4 address with CIDR prefix)

Note: Wildcard mask is the inverse of subnet mask (e.g., 255.255.255.0 → 0.0.0.255)

Wildcard Mask to Subnet

Format: network_ip wildcard_mask (e.g., 10.0.0.0 0.0.0.255)

Wildcard Mask to CIDR

Format: network_ip wildcard_mask (e.g., 10.0.0.0 0.0.0.255)
